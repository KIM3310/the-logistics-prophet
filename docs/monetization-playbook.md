# Monetization Playbook - the-logistics-prophet

This is a commercial planning artifact, not a revenue guarantee. It exists to turn an archived technical proof into a clear, ethical selling motion.

## Commercial Lane

- **Lane:** Logistics prediction and operations control tower
- **Primary buyer:** 3PL operators, manufacturers, distribution networks, logistics SaaS teams, and operations consultancies.
- **Why this can sell:** Logistics teams need delay prediction, explainability, data quality, and operational action boards in one reviewable loop.

## Offer Ladder

1. **Control-tower assessment** - $8k-$25k. Review current delay data, incident flow, and evidence-pack posture.
2. **Lane-specific prediction pilot** - $35k-$120k. Adapt the model, SHAP evidence, and operations console to one logistics lane.
3. **Operations analytics retainer** - $8k-$30k/month. Maintain quality gates, scorecards, and action-impact reviews.

## Acquisition Channels

- Logistics operations content
- Operations consultancy partnerships
- Before/after action-board case studies

## Proof Gates Before Selling

- Synthetic data is clearly marked
- Evidence pack export works
- Optional integrations remain optional

## Risk Boundaries

- Do not claim production accuracy without customer data
- Keep operational recommendations human-reviewed
- Avoid implying live carrier integrations in static demos

## 30/60/90 Day GTM

- **30 days:** Publish a delay-cost calculator
- **60 days:** Record a control-tower walkthrough
- **90 days:** Offer a paid lane assessment

## Metrics That Matter

- Assessment starts
- Delay triage time saved
- Prediction calibration
- Retainer conversion

## Positioning Note

Keep the repository's archived status visible. Sell the reusable workflow, diagnostic, enablement material, or pilot path that the proof makes credible; do not sell the archive as an unsupported production service.
