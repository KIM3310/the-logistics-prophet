# Revenue Growth Model - The Logistics Prophet

Updated: 2026-05-30

This note translates the archived proof into a current, ethical revenue path. It keeps the repository's archived status visible while preserving the commercial idea behind the work.

## Model

| Field | Decision |
|---|---|
| Revenue type | Archived proof to diagnostic, workshop, enablement, pilot, or partner package |
| Primary reader or buyer | 3PL operators, manufacturers, distribution networks, logistics SaaS teams, and operations consultancies. |
| Wedge | Logistics teams need delay prediction, explainability, data quality, and operational action boards in one reviewable loop. |
| Operating note | Sell the revived workflow or advisory package; do not sell an unsupported archive as a live product. |

## Offer Ladder

| Offer | Price assumption | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Control-tower assessment | $8k-$25k | Review current delay data, incident flow, and evidence-pack posture. |
| Lane-specific prediction pilot | $35k-$120k | Adapt the model, SHAP evidence, and operations console to one logistics lane. |
| Operations analytics retainer | $8k-$30k/month | Maintain quality gates, scorecards, and action-impact reviews. |

## Activation Loop

- Traffic source: Logistics operations content.
- First action: read the README status note and one proof path that can be checked quickly.
- Conversion offer: Control-tower assessment.
- Expansion: convert a diagnostic or workshop into a scoped pilot, enablement pack, or maintained partner asset.

## Growth Experiments

- Publish a delay-cost calculator
- Record a control-tower walkthrough
- Offer a paid lane assessment

## Metrics

- Assessment starts
- Delay triage time saved
- Prediction calibration
- Retainer conversion

## Ethical Boundary

- Do not claim production accuracy without customer data
- Keep operational recommendations human-reviewed
- Avoid implying live carrier integrations in static demos
- Keep archived status visible in sales or portfolio conversations.
- Do not invent users, revenue, endorsements, certifications, or customer traction.
- Use buyer psychology only to reduce confusion and risk, not to pressure users.
